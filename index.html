Memory Card Game

Sharpen your memory!
Flip the cards, find the matching pairs, and complete the board as fast as you can.

Game Rules

  • Click a card to reveal its picture.
  • Match two cards to keep them open.
  • If they don’t match, they flip back.
  • Every 2 cards flipped - 1 move.
  • Score: Fewer moves - higher score.
  • Beat the timer and match all pairs!
  • Congratulations! message appears when done.
